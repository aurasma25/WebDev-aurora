5 FACTS ABOUT MY DREAM HOLIDAY COUNTRIES

1. Australia

Australia is famous for kangaroos and koalas.

2. Paris, France

Paris is known as the City of Love and has the Eiffel Tower.

3. Japan

Japan is famous for cherry blossoms and delicious sushi.

4. Switzerland

Switzerland is famous for beautiful mountains and snowy landscapes.

5. Italy

Italy is famous for pizza, pasta, and the historic Colosseum.
